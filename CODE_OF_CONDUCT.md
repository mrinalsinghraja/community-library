# Code of conduct

This project exists so a community can run a free library for its children. The
people who use it, volunteer for it and contribute to it should all be treated
with kindness and respect, in the code review as much as at the library desk.

## What we expect

- Be kind. Assume good intent, and say what you mean plainly and politely.
- Be patient with people who are new to the project or to open source.
- Give feedback on the work, not the person. Accept feedback the same way.
- Protect children's privacy. Never post a child's name, photograph, family
  details or anything else that could identify them, anywhere in this project.
- Respect that volunteers give their time. A slow reply is not a refusal.

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, insults, threats or personal attacks.
- Discrimination or demeaning comments about anyone's age, gender, religion,
  caste, disability, race, nationality, language or background.
- Unwelcome attention, or sharing someone's private information without their
  permission.
- Anything that sexualises or endangers a child.
- Spam, advertising unrelated to the project, or deliberately disrupting
  discussions.

## Where this applies

In this repository: issues, pull requests, discussions, commit messages and any
other space where you take part in the project.

## Reporting a problem

Email **mrinalsinghraja@gmail.com**. Reports are read in confidence, and the
person reporting is not named to anyone else without their agreement. Security
problems follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md) instead.

## What happens next

A maintainer will reply, look at what happened, and decide what to do. That can
be a private word, a request to edit or remove a comment, a temporary break from
the project, or a permanent ban from it. Content that harms children is removed
immediately.
